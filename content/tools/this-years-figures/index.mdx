# This year's figures

This page is the one place on the site that prints figures set by law, regulation, or a federal agency: limits, rates, thresholds, ages, and deadlines. The knowledge-area pages name each concept in plain language and link here, so a figure changes in one place.

Every value carries the year it applies to and a link to its primary source. A figure fixed by statute shows "Set by statute" in the Year column. A figure that differs from state to state shows "Varies by state" and points you to where to look. A figure that is fixed by statute but still shows a year, such as the required-withdrawal starting age or the cap on the state and local tax deduction, is one where the statute itself sets a different figure for different years. "Monthly" in the Year column means the figure comes from a live release, so no number is printed here. Each figure appears once, under the knowledge area it fits best; if you do not see it under the area you came from, use your browser's find. Every value and link on this page was checked against its source in September 2026. The page is reviewed once a year.
