# This year's figures

This page is the one place on the site that prints figures set by law, regulation, or a federal agency: limits, rates, thresholds, ages, and deadlines. The knowledge-area pages name each concept in plain language and link here, so a figure changes in one place.

Every value carries the year it applies to and a link to its primary source. A figure fixed by statute shows "Set by statute" in the Year column. A figure that differs from state to state shows "Varies by state" and points you to where to look. Each figure appears once, under the knowledge area it fits best; if you do not see it under the area you came from, use your browser's find. A cell that reads VERIFY is waiting on a check against its source. The page is reviewed once a year.

Last reviewed: September 2026
