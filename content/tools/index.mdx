# Tools

This section holds the calculators, checklists, and worksheets that turn the planning process into something you can do at a desk. Each tool supports a specific step and explains what to do with the result.

## What belongs here

A calculator does the arithmetic behind a decision, such as how long savings will last at a given rate of spending. A checklist makes sure nothing is skipped when a step has many parts. A worksheet gives you a structure for gathering facts, so the numbers you carry into the next step are complete and consistent.

## Being built

This section is under construction. One tool is live, and more are coming.

**[This year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures)** is available now. It lists every limit, rate, threshold, and age the knowledge-area pages refer to, with the year each applies to and its primary source, and is refreshed once a year.

The tools planned next are:

- **A net worth worksheet.** You list everything you own and everything you owe. The difference between the two is your net worth, and it is the single figure that best summarizes where you stand today.
- **A cash flow worksheet.** You record the money coming in and the money going out over a month or a year. Cash flow shows whether your plan can be funded from what you already earn.
- **A goals checklist.** It walks you through making each goal specific enough to plan for: what it is, how much it costs, when you need it, and how it ranks against your other goals.

Each will be added here as it is ready.
